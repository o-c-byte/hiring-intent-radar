    How it works

    Score

    Each distinct move (one per signal type and target country) adds strength × recency: strong 3, medium 2, weak 1; recency 1.0 for postings up to 30 days old, 0.6 up to 90, 0.3 after. Hiring volume adds open roles ÷ 25, capped at 2. Additional postings behind the same move add no points, and market expansion scores in three countries at most.

    Limits

    • The data is a single snapshot. "Only role in Italy" describes today's board and does not prove a first hire.
    • Companies cancel some roles before filling them and keep others open indefinitely, so a posting shows intent and the hire may never happen.
    • Companies that hire through in-house or closed applicant tracking systems do not appear.
    • Each card names the ATS field behind its date. Some systems reset that date when a company reposts a role.
